Our Story

Hey there 👋, I’m Yaron! I’m currently pursuing a graduate degreein Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence, specializing in natural language processing (NLP), the field powering today’s most advanced AI systems, including large language models like ChatGPT.

What sets ResumaticAI apart is not just the use of AI, but how that AI is built and applied. My background allows me to go beyond off-the-shelf solutions and deeply understand how language models behave, where they fall short, and how to optimize them for specific, high-stakes tasks like resume writing.

Resumes are not just text, they’re strategic documents that need to communicate impact, align with recruiter expectations, and pass applicant tracking systems. Achieving this requires more than generic AI prompts. It requires carefully selected models, precise prompt engineering, and an understanding of how subtle language choices influence outcomes.

That’s exactly where my expertise comes in. Every improvement generated by ResumaticAI is informed by advanced knowledge of LLM behavior, fine-tuning strategies, and real-world NLP applications, resulting in outputs that are more targeted, more effective, and more aligned with what actually gets results.

At this stage I do not yet have the resources to invest in creating a flashy, award-winning interface, however this is more than made up for by the depth of my expertise in large language models and the quality of the results they produce. Because, at the end of the day, the value isn’t in how the tool looks, it’s in how much stronger it makes your resume.
