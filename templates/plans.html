FAQ

What happens after the 7 day trial?

If you don’t cancel before the end of the trial, your card authorization is captured as your first month’s payment and your subscription renews. We offer a free trial, because we believe you will find having access to unlimited tailored resumes, a job search coach, and the job search hub, as being indispensable for an effective job search campaign.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade or switch billing options anytime.

Is payment secure?

Use a trusted processor (Stripe/PayPal) and serve everything over HTTPS.

Do you offer a satisfaction guarantee?

Yes, if you’re not satisfied, contact support and we’ll help you get it resolved. If it’s still not a fit, we’ll refund your most recent payment.
