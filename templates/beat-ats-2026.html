What Is an ATS and Why Does It Matter?

An Applicant Tracking System is software that companies use to collect, organize, and filter job applications. Think of it as a gatekeeper between you and the hiring manager.

Popular ATS platforms include Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, iCIMS, and Taleo. Each works slightly differently, but they all share a common approach:

  1. Parse your resume - extract your name, contact info, experience, education, and skills into structured data fields
  2. Match keywords - compare the content of your resume against the job description
  3. Rank candidates - score and sort applicants based on how well they match

The ranking isn't subjective. It's algorithmic. And that means you can optimize for it.

7 Strategies to Beat ATS in 2026

1. Use the Exact Keywords from the Job Description

This is the single most impactful thing you can do. ATS software matches your resume content against the language in the job posting.

How to do it right:

  • Read the job description carefully and identify the specific skills, tools, and qualifications mentioned
  • Mirror the exact phrasing
  • Include both the spelled-out version and abbreviation: "Search Engine Optimization (SEO)" covers both
  • Place keywords in your experience section (in context), skills section, and summary

Don't overdo it. Keyword stuffing can hurt you.

2. Choose the Right File Format

  • Safest choice: .docx - universally parsed by every major ATS
  • Usually fine: .pdf - most modern ATS platforms parse these correctly
  • Avoid: .pages, .odt, image-based PDFs

3. Use Standard Section Headers

Use these: Work Experience, Education, Skills, Summary, Certifications, Projects

Avoid these: "My Journey", "What I Bring to the Table", "Career Highlights"

4. Keep Formatting Simple and Clean

Safe formatting:

  • Standard fonts (Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman, Garamond)
  • Single-column layout
  • Standard bullet points
  • Bold and italic for emphasis (sparingly)

Formatting that breaks ATS:

  • Tables, text boxes, or columns
  • Headers/footers
  • Images, icons, logos, or graphics
  • Custom fonts or symbols
  • Infographics, charts, or skill bars

5. Quantify Your Achievements

Weak: "Responsible for social media management"

Strong: "Grew Instagram following from 2,400 to 18,500 in 8 months, increasing website referral traffic by 34%"

6. Tailor Your Resume for Every Application

A single generic resume might score 40-60% on keyword matching. A tailored resume can hit 80-95%.

FAQ

How do I know if a company uses ATS?

If you're applying through an online portal, the company almost certainly uses ATS.

Can I trick ATS by hiding keywords in white text?

No. Modern ATS systems detect hidden text.

Should I use a resume template?

Yes, as long as it's ATS-friendly.

How many keywords should I include?

Focus on matching the specific skills, tools, and qualifications mentioned in the job description.

What's the difference between ATS optimization and keyword stuffing?

ATS optimization means naturally incorporating relevant keywords in context. Keyword stuffing means cramming terms in where they don't belong.
